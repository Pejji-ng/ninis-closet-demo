Our story

Sunday should feel like a hug.

nini's closet started in a small Lagos apartment with one rule: every piece should make the wearer want to slow down. No itchy fabrics, no fussy ties, no "just for the photo" loungewear that lives in the drawer.

Each set is sourced and quality-checked by hand. We work with small mills and ethical makers across West Africa, prioritizing fabric feel over flashy branding. The result is loungewear that feels older than it is, in the best way.

If you've ever wished Sunday lasted a little longer, this is for you.

— Nini
